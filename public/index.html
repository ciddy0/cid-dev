A schematic of the LA Metro rail network, drawn from Metro's published timetable data and straightened onto 45° and 90° bends.

  • View pulls back to the whole network.
  • Drag to move around, pinch or scroll to zoom.
  • Tap or hover a station for its name and lines.
  • Tab steps through stations along each line.
  • The arrow in the corner (or Esc) brings you back here.
